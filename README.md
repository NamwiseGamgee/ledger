# Ledger

A simple, calm monthly budget app that runs in your browser and installs on your phone like a regular app. No account, no sign-up, and your data never leaves your device.

**Open the app:** https://namwisegamgee.github.io/ledger/

## Features

- **Month-by-month tracking.** Each month gets its own summary, and a fresh month starts automatically when a new one begins.
- **Multiple paychecks.** Add salary as many times as you like, which suits weekly or biweekly pay.
- **Expenses by category.** Grocery, Rent, Restaurants, Car maintenance, and Other expense.
- **Deposits.** Log extra money like refunds, gifts, or side income.
- **Notes on every entry.** Add a note to remember what each expense or deposit was for.
- **Monthly breakdown.** See how much went to each category, how many paychecks came in, and your net savings.
- **Total savings.** The homepage shows your combined savings across all finished months.
- **Saved automatically.** Entries stay on your device until you delete them, even after closing or refreshing.
- **Works offline.** Once installed, it opens without an internet connection.
- **Backup and restore.** Download a backup file and restore it on another device.
- **Light and dark mode.** Follows your device's setting.

## Install it on your phone

**iPhone:** Open the link above in Safari, tap the Share button, choose **Add to Home Screen**, then tap **Add**. Always open Ledger from its home screen icon so your data stays in one place.

**Android:** Open the link in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, and choose **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.

**Mac:** Open the link in Safari and choose **File → Add to Dock**. In Chrome, use the install option in the address bar or menu.

## Privacy

Everything you enter is stored only on your own device. Nothing is sent to a server, and no one else, including the owner of this repository, can see your data. Each device keeps its own separate copy, so use backup and restore to move entries between devices.
