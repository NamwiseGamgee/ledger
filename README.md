# Ledger

A simple monthly budget app that runs in your browser and installs on your phone like a regular app. No account, no sign-up, and your data never leaves your device.

**Open the app:** https://namwisegamgee.github.io/ledger/

## Features

- **Monthly tracking.** Log salary (as many paychecks as you like), expenses, and deposits, each with an optional note. A fresh month starts automatically.
- **Monthly breakdown.** See spending by category, money in and out, and net savings for any month.
- **Total savings.** Combined savings from every finished month, shown on the home screen.
- **Custom categories.** Add, rename, delete, and color categories with any color you like.
- **All-time insights.** See what share of your income went to each category, plus your monthly averages.
- **Search.** Find entries by category, note, or person, grouped by month. Not case-sensitive.
- **Loans.** Track money you lend and borrow with anyone. Record partial payments or mark loans as paid, with a full history. Loans count toward your monthly totals only when money actually changes hands.
- **Edit anything.** Fix or delete any entry, and all totals update automatically.
- **Works offline.** Saved on your device until you delete it, with backup and restore.

## How to use it

- Tap **+ Salary**, **− Expense**, or **+ Deposit** to add an entry. Tap any entry to edit or delete it.
- Tap **See breakdown** or a past month to see its details.
- Tap **Owed to you** to track loans, and **All-time insights** to see trends or search.
- Tap the gear icon to manage categories, change the currency, or back up your data.

## Install it

**iPhone:** Open the link in Safari, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**. Always open Ledger from that icon so your data stays in one place.

**Android:** Open the link in Chrome, tap ⋮, then **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.

**Mac:** In Safari, choose **File → Add to Dock**. In Chrome, use the install option in the address bar.
**Windows:** Open the link in Edge or Chrome, then click the install icon in the address bar (or in Edge, go to **⋯ → Apps → Install this site as an app**).

## Privacy

Everything is stored only on your device. Nothing is sent to a server, and no one else, including the owner of this repository, can see your data. Each device keeps its own copy, so use backup and restore to move data between devices.
